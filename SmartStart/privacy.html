Privacy notice

SMART START · Version 1.2, 5 November 2026 · Written to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

On this page

    1. Who we are

    SMART START is a job-search tracker with an artificial intelligence (AI) assistant called Stuart. It is developed and run by Muhammad Alif, an independent developer ("we", "us" in this notice). Muhammad Alif is also the Data Protection Officer (DPO) and can be reached at muhammad.alif@me.com.

    This notice explains what personal data we collect when you use SMART START, why, where it is processed, how long we keep it and the choices you have. You must be 18 or over to use SMART START.

    2. What we collect

    • Account details: your email address, your sign-in method (email and password, or Google), whether your email address is verified, and when you signed up and last signed in. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile details from Google; we never see your Google password. Passwords for email sign-in are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are not visible to us.
    • Your tracker records: the applications you add or import (organisation, role, channel, dates, stage and stage history, notes, next actions, scores, salary details if you enter them, and the names of referees or contacts you choose to record), deadlines and your dashboard settings.
    • Documents you upload: curricula vitae (CVs), resumes, cover letters, forms and job advertisements, in the form you upload them, with a document index (file name, type, version, size, date and the applications they belong to).
    • Stuart's work, if you use Stuart: the text extracted from your CV, the profile Stuart builds from it (roles, dates, skills, qualifications), your stated goal and target role, your plan, your master CV (as you edit it in the app, or as Stuart drafts it from your answers when you build a CV from scratch, with those answers), the advertisements you ask Stuart to check (as text, a link Stuart fetches, a file or a screenshot), each fit check with its scores and likelihood band, the tailored CV, resume and cover letter drafts Stuart builds for a job, Stuart's inspection report on them and the Word files you save, the rules you set for your documents and your answers to Stuart's questions about them, your chat messages with Stuart and its replies, and the record that you agreed to Stuart's use of AI and when.
    • Usage records: how much of your monthly Stuart allowance you have used and how many requests you made, and technical error logs. Error logs do not contain the content of your documents or messages.
    • Feedback, if you send it: your message and its kind (something broke, an idea or a question), your sign-in email so we can reply, and, if you leave the box ticked, the app version, screen size and device type. Identification numbers are removed before it is saved.

    We do not ask for, and you should not upload, National Registration Identity Card (NRIC) or Foreign Identification Number (FIN) numbers, bank details or health information. See section 9.

    3. Why we use it

    • To provide the service you signed up for: storing your applications, stages, deadlines and documents, and showing your dashboard.
    • If you choose to use Stuart: to read your CV, draft your plan, check advertisements against your CV, answer your questions and suggest stage updates from news you give it.
    • To keep the service secure, enforce the per-account AI allowance, fix faults, and respond to your requests and complaints.
    • To tell you about important changes to the service, this notice or the terms of use.

    We use your data only for these purposes, with your consent (given when you sign up, and separately for Stuart) or where the PDPA otherwise allows. We do not sell your data, we show no advertising, and SMART START contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

    4. Stuart and AI processing

    Stuart is available to every account with a confirmed email address, and only after you agree, on a separate screen, to your documents and advertisements being sent to the AI service. That consent is separate from your agreement at sign-up. You can withdraw it at any time (section 7); the tracker keeps working without Stuart.

    When you ask Stuart to do something, it sends only the content needed for that request to the Claude application programming interface (API) run by Anthropic:

    • reading your CV: the CV text (or, for a scanned Portable Document Format (PDF) file with no text layer, the file itself);
    • your plan: your profile, goal, target role and start date;
    • building your CV from scratch: your answers in that interview and the draft so far;
    • improving a part of your CV: that part and your CV text;
    • checking a job: the advertisement (text, the text of the linked page, a file or a screenshot) and your CV text (or your master CV);
    • building documents for a job: your master CV, your CV text, your rules, the job details, the fit check and any answers you give Stuart;
    • inspecting the built documents: the documents, your master CV, your CV text, your rules and the fit check;
    • reading a rule you type: the rule as you typed it; checking your CV for things that disagree: your master CV and your CV text;
    • chat: your message, recent messages in that conversation, the job details, the fit check, your profile and your plan;
    • logging an update: what you tell Stuart and the list of your applications (organisation, role, current stage and date applied).

    Before your CV text, your interview answers and your update messages are sent, Stuart removes anything in the format of an NRIC or FIN number. This cannot be done for scanned PDFs, screenshots and images, which are sent as they are; the app tells you when a file could not be screened.

    Stuart gives a likelihood band (low, medium or high) with its reasons, never a probability of being hired. Everything Stuart writes is a draft for you to check. Stuart never contacts employers, recruiters or referees; anything you send, you send yourself.

    6. How long we keep it

    • Your records, documents and Stuart's work are kept until you delete them or close your account.
    • Accounts with no sign-in for 24 months are closed and their data deleted, after 30 days' notice by email.
    • Feedback you send from the app is kept for up to 24 months, and deleted earlier if you close your account.
    • When you delete something or close your account, it is removed from our live systems at once. Copies may remain in our providers' back-ups for a limited period under Google's and Anthropic's own terms, after which they are overwritten.
    • Usage records for the AI allowance are kept with your account and deleted when it is closed.

    7. Your rights and choices

    • Access: everything we hold about you is visible in the app. You can also ask the DPO for a copy and for information on how your data has been used or disclosed in the past year.
    • Correction: change an application's stage in the app, upload a corrected CV for Stuart to read again, or ask Stuart to redraft your plan. For anything you cannot change yourself, ask the DPO and we will correct it.
    • Export: Settings lets you download all your records as a JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) file, your applications as a comma-separated values (CSV) file, a printable report, and your dates as a calendar file.
    • Withdrawal of consent: to stop Stuart processing your data, write to the DPO; we will switch Stuart off for your account and tell you what that means for the service. Withdrawing consent to the service as a whole means closing your account.
    • Deletion: delete an application from its window in Applications, or a job from its page in Stuart; you choose whether its files in Documents and Stuart's work for it go too, and you can undo for a few seconds. To delete everything, close your account in Settings: that deletes your records (everything under your account in Cloud Firestore), all your files in Cloud Storage and your sign-in account. You can also ask the DPO to delete particular records or files.

    How to make a request: email muhammad.alif@me.com from the address you use to sign in, saying what you need. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible and within the time the PDPA requires (for access and correction, within 30 days, or we will tell you within that time when we can respond). We may charge a reasonable fee for an access request only where the PDPA allows, and will tell you beforehand.

    Complaints: please contact the DPO first. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) of Singapore.

    8. How we protect your data

    • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers.
    • Database and storage rules allow each account to read and write only its own records and files. The AI allowance ledger can be written only by our server code.
    • Only the developer has administrative access to the systems that run SMART START. We do not browse your CVs, chats or documents. We open an account's content only if you ask us to fix a problem with it, or if the law requires it. Stuart's automated processing, described in section 4, is how your documents are read to give you results.
    • The AI service key is held in Google Secret Manager and never reaches the app or your device.
    • The app can blur itself in the app switcher and signs you out after a period without use that you choose in Settings (5 to 60 minutes). No web app can prevent screenshots or screen recording on your device.

    Data breaches: if a breach occurs, we will assess it promptly. If it is notifiable under the PDPA, we will notify the PDPC within 3 calendar days of assessing it as notifiable, and notify affected users as the PDPA requires.

    9. Your responsibilities

    • Upload only documents you have the right to use.
    • If your records name other people, such as referees or contacts, make sure you have their permission to record their details.
    • Do not upload other people's identification numbers, and remove your own from documents where you can.

    10. Data on your device

    If you keep "Keep an offline copy on this device" on (Settings, Privacy), a copy of your records is cached in your browser so the app opens without a connection. Your browser also stores your sign-in session and a few preferences (theme, filters, pace target). Settings, "Clear this device's copy", signs you out and removes the cached records. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

    11. Changes to this notice

    We will announce changes in the app, with the new version and date, before they take effect. If a change affects how your data is used in a significant way, we will ask for your consent again.
