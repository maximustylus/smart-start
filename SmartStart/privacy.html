Privacy notice

SMART START · Version 1.5.8, 13 November 2026 · Written to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

On this page

    1. Who we are

    SMART START is a job-search tracker with an artificial intelligence (AI) assistant called Stuart. It is developed and run by Firstletter Lab, the trading name of Muhammad Alif, an independent developer ("we", "us" in this notice). The business is not yet registered. Muhammad Alif is also the Data Protection Officer (DPO) and can be reached at muhammad.alif@me.com.

    This notice explains what personal data we collect when you use SMART START, why, where it is processed, how long we keep it and the choices you have. You must be 18 or over to use SMART START.

    2. What we collect

    • Account details: your email address, your sign-in method (email and password, or Google), whether your email address is verified, and when you signed up and last signed in. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile details from Google; we never see your Google password. Passwords for email sign-in are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are not visible to us.
    • Your tracker records: the applications you add or import (organisation, role, channel, dates, stage and stage history, notes, next actions, scores, salary details if you enter them, and the names of referees or contacts you choose to record), deadlines and your dashboard settings.
    • Your photo, if you add one: one small picture, with a round copy of it, kept in your account for the CVs and resumes where you switch it on. Like your documents, it opens through a private link that only the app is given. It is not sent to the AI service. Removing it deletes it, but a CV or resume you already saved keeps its copy until you delete that document. It is deleted when you close your account.
    • Your signature, if you add one: the signature you draw or photograph, cleaned on your device so only the ink is kept, without location or camera details. It is kept in your account and can be read only by our server code, which adds it to a cover letter when you switch it on for that letter. It is never sent to the AI service and never made by it. Deleting it removes it at once, but a cover letter you already saved or downloaded keeps its copy, kept like your other documents, until you delete that document. It is deleted when you close your account.
    • Documents you upload: curricula vitae (CVs), resumes, cover letters, forms and job advertisements, in the form you upload them, with a document index (file name, type, version, size, date and the applications they belong to).
    • Stuart's work, if you use Stuart: the application emails Stuart drafts for you, with the address each is going to; your interview practice questions, answers, how you said they felt and feedback, the text extracted from your CV, the profile Stuart builds from it (roles, dates, skills, qualifications), your stated goal and target role, your plan and up to six earlier versions of it, your master CV (as you edit it in the app, or as Stuart drafts it from your answers when you build a CV from scratch, with those answers), the advertisements you ask Stuart to check (as text, a link Stuart fetches, a file or a screenshot), each fit check with its scores and likelihood band, the tailored CV, resume and cover letter drafts Stuart builds for a job, the general resume and any CV, resume or cover letter Stuart makes for you without a job (from 13 November 2026), with the line you type to say who a document is for (a message or advertisement you paste for such a cover letter is sent to the AI service to write that letter and is not kept), Stuart's review of your closed applications (from 13 November 2026, only when you ask for it in Insights: for each application that was unsuccessful, had no reply or reached an interview, the role title, the level and sector, how you applied, the stage it reached, its fit check results and anything you wrote about what the employer said are sent to the AI service; the organisation field is not sent and its name is removed from the role title, how you applied, the requirements and your note, though a very short name, or one written in another form, may remain; the review is kept with your account until you run it again), Stuart's inspection report on them and the Word files you save, the rules you set for your documents and your answers to Stuart's questions about them (from 12 November 2026 kept across jobs, each with its date and the job it was given for, so Stuart does not ask again; you see, change and remove them in the CV builder under "What you've told Stuart"), your chat messages with Stuart and its replies, and the record that you agreed to Stuart's use of AI and when.
    • Credits: your balance, what you unlocked, and a history of grants and spending, including the organisation of the job. A record under a scrambled form of your email address shows that your mailbox has had the free credits; it is kept after an account closes and deleted once the free offer has ended.
    • Notifications in the app: a short list of when Stuart finished work for you, naming the organisation, and which notices you have already seen.
    • Notifications on your devices, if you turn them on: for each device, the address its push service gives us and the keys to reach it, a label (for example iPhone) and when it last received a message; for your account, your choices of summary time and details, and when the last summary was sent. Messages carry organisation names and your own short notes (a date you added, a next step), or nothing named if you switch details off; never your documents, CV or job advertisements.
    • Features you ask to hear about: if you tap Notify me on a feature that is coming soon, we keep that choice and may email you once when it is ready.
    • Usage records: how much of your monthly Stuart allowance you have used and how many requests you made, a record of that month's use held under a scrambled (hashed) form of your email address, so that one mailbox has one allowance across its accounts and aliases, and technical error logs. Error logs do not contain the content of your documents or messages.
    • Feedback, if you send it: your message and its kind (something broke, an idea or a question), your sign-in email so we can reply, and, if you leave the box ticked, the app version, screen size and device type. Identification numbers are removed before it is saved.

    We do not ask for, and you should not upload, National Registration Identity Card (NRIC) or Foreign Identification Number (FIN) numbers, bank details or health information. See section 9.

    3. Why we use it

    • To provide the service you signed up for: storing your applications, stages, deadlines and documents, and showing your dashboard.
    • If you choose to use Stuart: to read your CV, draft and review your plan, check advertisements against your CV, answer your questions, help you practise for interviews, suggest stage updates from news you give it, and suggest a sector for your applications when you ask.
    • To keep the service secure, enforce the per-account AI allowance, fix faults, and respond to your requests and complaints.
    • If you add a signature: to put it on the cover letters you choose to sign.
    • If you turn on notifications on a device: to send you a daily summary and tell you when Stuart has finished.
    • To tell you about important changes to the service, this notice or the terms of use.

    We use your data only for these purposes, with your consent (given when you sign up, and separately for Stuart) or where the PDPA otherwise allows. We do not sell your data, we show no advertising, and SMART START contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

    4. Stuart and AI processing

    Stuart is available to every account with a confirmed email address, and only after you agree, on a separate screen, to your documents and advertisements being sent to the AI service. That consent is separate from your agreement at sign-up. You can withdraw it at any time (section 7); the tracker keeps working without Stuart.

    When you ask Stuart to do something, it sends only the content needed for that request to the Claude application programming interface (API) run by Anthropic:

    • reading your CV: the CV text (or, for a scanned Portable Document Format (PDF) file with no text layer, the file itself);
    • your plan: your profile, goal, target role and start date;
    • reviewing your plan, when you ask: your plan, your goal, target role and start date, and counts from your applications (how many are at each stage, replies, interviews, by sector and when sent), never organisation names, notes or documents;
    • building your CV from scratch: your answers in that interview and the draft so far;
    • improving a part of your CV: that part and your CV text;
    • checking your CV for repeats: the headline, summary and bullets of your master CV, with each role's title and organisation, as on screen;
    • checking a job: the advertisement (text, the text of the linked page, a file or a screenshot), your CV text, your master CV and your saved answers to Stuart's questions;
    • drafting an application email: your name, your rules, the job details and your cover letter;
    • suggesting sectors (Insights, Where to aim): the organisation and role of up to 60 applications without a confirmed sector, and nothing else;
    • building documents for a job: your master CV, your CV text, your rules, the job details, the fit check, any answers you give Stuart and your saved answers from earlier jobs;
    • inspecting the built documents: the documents, your master CV, your CV text, your rules and the fit check;
    • reading a rule you type: the rule as you typed it; checking your CV for things that disagree: your master CV and your CV text;
    • practising for an interview: your typed answer and any earlier attempt at that question, how you said it felt, the job details, the fit check, your master CV, your rules and what you decided in coaching;
    • chat: your message, recent messages in that conversation, the job details, the fit check, your profile and your plan;
    • coaching you on a job: your messages in that conversation, the job details, the fit check, your master CV, your profile and your saved answers to Stuart's questions;
    • logging an update: what you tell Stuart and the list of your applications (organisation, role, current stage and date applied).

    Before your CV text, your interview answers and your update messages are sent, Stuart removes anything in the format of an NRIC or FIN number. This cannot be done for scanned PDFs, screenshots and images, which are sent as they are; the app tells you when a file could not be screened.

    Stuart gives a likelihood band (low, medium or high) with its reasons, never a probability of being hired. Everything Stuart writes is a draft for you to check. Stuart never contacts employers, recruiters or referees; anything you send, you send yourself.

    6. How long we keep it

    • Daily usage totals by feature, with no account named: up to 24 months.
    • Your records, documents and Stuart's work are kept until you delete them or close your account.
    • Accounts with no use for 24 months are closed and their data deleted, after 30 days' notice by email. A daily check does this.
    • A device's notification details are deleted when you turn notifications off on it, use Sign out or "Clear this device's copy" on it, when its push service reports it gone, or when you close your account. Being signed out after a period without use, or locking the app, keeps notifications on.
    • Feedback you send from the app is kept for up to 24 months, and deleted earlier if you close your account.
    • When you delete something or close your account, it is removed from our live systems at once. Copies may remain in our providers' back-ups for a limited period under Google's and Anthropic's own terms, after which they are overwritten.
    • Usage records for the AI allowance are kept with your account and deleted when it is closed, except the month's use held under a scrambled form of your email address. That record stays until the calendar month has ended, then a daily check deletes it, so that closing an account and opening another does not reset the allowance. It holds no address in readable form and no document content. The short email-limit record likewise lapses within about a day instead of being deleted at closure.

    7. Your rights and choices

    • Access: you can see your applications, documents, master CV, jobs and Stuart's work in the app. A few records are not shown there, such as usage records, the short email-limit record, the notice record for inactive accounts, older jobs beyond the latest 50, and the original CV and advertisement files you gave Stuart. You can ask the DPO for a copy of anything we hold, and for information on how your data has been used or disclosed in the past year.
    • Correction: change an application's stage in the app, upload a corrected CV for Stuart to read again, or edit your plan, change its direction or ask Stuart to redraft it. For anything you cannot change yourself, ask the DPO and we will correct it.
    • Export: Settings lets you download your records and Stuart's work as a JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) file (the files in Documents download separately from there; for the original CV and advertisement files you gave Stuart, ask the DPO), your applications as a comma-separated values (CSV) file, a printable report, and your dates as a calendar file.
    • Withdrawal of consent: to stop Stuart processing your data, write to the DPO; we will switch Stuart off for your account and tell you what that means for the service. Withdrawing consent to the service as a whole means closing your account.
    • Deletion: delete an application from its window in Applications, or a job from its page in Stuart; you choose whether its files in Documents and Stuart's work for it go too, and you can undo for a few seconds. In Documents, Select lets you delete one file or many, with the same few seconds to undo. To delete everything, close your account in Settings: that deletes your records (everything under your account in Cloud Firestore), all your files in Cloud Storage and your sign-in account. You can also ask the DPO to delete particular records or files.

    How to make a request: email muhammad.alif@me.com from the address you use to sign in, saying what you need. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible and within the time the PDPA requires (for access and correction, within 30 days, or we will tell you within that time when we can respond). We may charge a reasonable fee for an access request only where the PDPA allows, and will tell you beforehand.

    Complaints: please contact the DPO first. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) of Singapore.

    8. How we protect your data

    • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers.
    • Database and storage rules allow each account to read and write only its own records and files. The AI allowance ledger can be written only by our server code.
    • Only the developer has administrative access to the systems that run SMART START. To run and support the service, the developer sees a dashboard of usage for each account: a masked email address (shown in full when needed to help someone), when the account joined and was last seen, whether its email is confirmed and AI use was agreed to, how far it has got (CV set up, jobs checked, documents built, marked as applied), how many jobs and applications it has, and its AI calls, tokens, cost and share of its monthly AI limit. It shows no content. The developer can save these figures to a file (PDF or CSV) on his own device for running the service; such files carry only the masked address, counts, dates and costs, never content, and are not published. Daily totals by feature, with no account named, are kept for up to 24 months to understand use; on a quiet day a total may reflect one person's use. We do not browse your CVs, chats or documents. We open an account's content only if you ask us to fix a problem with it, or if the law requires it. Stuart's automated processing, described in section 4, is how your documents are read to give you results.
    • The AI service key, and the key that signs notifications, are held in Google Secret Manager and never reach the app or your device. Your saved signature, and device notification details, can be read and written only by our server code.
    • The app can blur itself in the app switcher and signs you out after a period without use that you choose in Settings (5 to 60 minutes). No web app can prevent screenshots or screen recording on your device.

    Data breaches: if a breach occurs, we will assess it promptly. If it is notifiable under the PDPA, we will notify the PDPC within 3 calendar days of assessing it as notifiable, and notify affected users as the PDPA requires.

    9. Your responsibilities

    • Upload only documents you have the right to use.
    • If your records name other people, such as referees or contacts, make sure you have their permission to record their details.
    • Do not upload other people's identification numbers, and remove your own from documents where you can.

    10. Data on your device

    If you keep "Offline copy" on (Settings, Privacy and security), a copy of your records is cached in your browser so the app opens without a connection. Your browser also stores your sign-in session and a few preferences (theme, filters, and a copy of your pace target, which is also kept in your account) and, if you turn on notifications on this device, a reference to that device's notification registration. Settings, "Clear this device's copy", signs you out and removes the cached records. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.
