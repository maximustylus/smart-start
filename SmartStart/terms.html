    Why SMART START exists

    I built SMART START to keep track of my own job applications: each one on a timeline, with the follow-up it needs and when. It was inspired by the Ready at Work workshop run by Yayasan MENDAKI, which I attended, and by a presentation from the Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA) on the Employment and Employability Institute (e2i) and the National Trades Union Congress (NTUC) AI Career Coach. SMART START is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any of these organisations.

    2. The service

    SMART START is a job-search tracker. You can record applications and their stages, keep your curricula vitae (CVs), resumes, cover letters and job advertisements with the applications they belong to, see your progress on a dashboard, and export your records. Stuart, an artificial intelligence (AI) assistant built on Anthropic's Claude models, can read your CV or build one with you from scratch, suggest stronger wording, draft a plan, check advertisements against your CV, build a tailored CV, resume and cover letter for a job, answer questions about a job, and suggest stage updates from news you give it.
